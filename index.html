Kids Points Tracker

Start each day at 20 points. Today's behaviour sets tomorrow's consequence.

How penalties work: 20+ = no penalty; 18–19 = lose 1 hour; 15–17 = lose 2 hours; 10–14 = lose 3 hours; 0–9 = one full gaming-free day. Hours are taken off the end of a gaming day. A full gaming-free day stays pending until you manually mark it served.
